FAST TRACK '98 — PREVIEW BUILD
Version: v2.1.2 — Pre-Social Corrections

HOW TO PLAY
1. Download Fast-Track-98-v2.1.2-Pre-Social-Corrections.html.
2. Double-click the HTML file.
3. The game runs in a modern desktop web browser. No installation is required.
4. Save data and high scores are stored locally in your browser on your device.

WHAT CHANGED IN v2.1.2
- Corrected clickable district outlines across the updated district scenes.
- Reworked Job Junction around two constant fast-food jobs plus four rotating non-career openings.
- Made postings respond to the player's current employment/workplace level.
- Restored the first-job safety net after an initial rejection.
- Preserved v2.1.1 Life Score/high scores and the existing financial, housing, vehicle, career, and CN&N systems.

STATUS
This is an in-development preview build from Uncle Bear Studios / Sweitzer Ventures. Social Life, multiplayer, balance, artwork, interface elements, and other features will continue to evolve during development.
