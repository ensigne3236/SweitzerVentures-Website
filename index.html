FireWatch SentinelBRDG Innovation Challenge · 2026

First place at the
BRDG Innovation Challenge.

$4,000 prize October 9, 2026

FireWatch Sentinel won first place and a $4,000 prize at the BRDG Innovation Challenge on October 9, 2026. The project brings together a student team from three California universities to explore new approaches to wildfire mitigation.

Watch the system explained ↓

Exploring earlier signs of wildfire risk.

Could sound and local environmental data reveal vegetation stress before ignition?

FireWatch Sentinel is a student innovation project exploring tree-level acoustic and microclimate sensing as a possible complement to existing wildfire-monitoring tools.

An early-stage research concept, not a commercial detection service.

01SenseStudy acoustic activity and local conditions.
02TestInvestigate whether patterns indicate vegetation stress.
03LearnEvaluate what the evidence could mean for wildfire mitigation.

The team

  • Brandon Sweitzer California State University, Fullerton (CSUF)
  • Adhithi Ramalingam University of California, Irvine (UCI)
  • Shoug Almutairi San José State University (SJSU)

Project coaches

Sami Beaini, Hector Castaneda, and Sneha Ramalia supported the team through the challenge with coaching and project guidance.
