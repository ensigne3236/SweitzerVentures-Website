FireWatch SentinelResearch & prototype development

Exploring earlier signs of wildfire risk.

Could sound and local environmental data reveal vegetation stress before ignition?

FireWatch Sentinel is a student innovation project exploring tree-level acoustic and microclimate sensing as a possible complement to existing wildfire-monitoring tools.

An early-stage research concept, not a commercial detection service.

01SenseStudy acoustic activity and local conditions.
02TestInvestigate whether patterns indicate vegetation stress.
03LearnEvaluate what the evidence could mean for wildfire mitigation.
